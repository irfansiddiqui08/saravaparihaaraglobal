About Sarava Parihaara

Rooted in care. Born from a journey.

Where traditional wisdom meets modern personal care. Before Sarava Parihaara became a brand, it was Dr. Humayra’s own story.

It started with my own journey

A 13-year-old searching for answers

My journey with hair care began when I was just 13 years old. I experienced hormonal challenges that affected my hair and, along the way, my confidence too. What followed was a long journey of treatment, learning, observation and searching for better ways to care for my hair.

There were moments when the journey felt difficult. But instead of giving up, I became more curious. I wanted to understand my hair. I wanted to understand my scalp. I wanted to understand ingredients. And most importantly, I wanted to find a way to care for myself.

That search eventually became my purpose.

The formula that started it all

2020: a hair oil, made for myself

It wasn’t created to start a company. It was created because I wanted to care for my own hair. I researched ingredients, experimented with formulations, observed my experience, made changes and kept learning.

There was no shortcut. There was only patience, experimentation, learning and मेहनत.

When I began experiencing positive changes in my own hair, something changed within me. I realised: what if the journey that gave me hope could become a journey I share with others?

That thought became the beginning of Sarava Parihaara.
