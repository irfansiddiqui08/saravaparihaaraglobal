Frequently asked questions

Can’t find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll reply personally.

How do I place an order?

Add products to your cart and tap “Checkout on WhatsApp”. Fill in your name, number and address, and WhatsApp opens with your full order typed in. Send it, and our team confirms payment and delivery with you directly. You can also tap “Buy on WhatsApp” on any product page.

What payment options do you accept?

Our team shares payment options when confirming your order on WhatsApp.

Do you deliver across India and abroad?

Yes. We ship across India, and we have customers in Dubai, Canada and other Gulf countries. For international orders, message us on WhatsApp for shipping charges and timelines.

Which product should I start with?

Most customers start with the Hair Regrowth Oil and Hair Growth Shampoo. Add the Maonic Hair Mask or 21 Herbs Heena once a week for a complete routine. For personal guidance, book a consultation or message us.

Which size of Hair Regrowth Oil should I buy?

The 100 ml bottle (₹599) is a good way to try the oil. The 200 ml bottle (₹999) is better value if you oil 2–3 times a week, as we recommend.

How soon will I see results?

Hair and skin care work with consistency, and every person is different. Follow the routine regularly, and feel free to share your progress with us on WhatsApp so we can guide you.

Are your products suitable for men?

Yes. Our hair-care range and the AuraGlow Skin Polish are for both men and women.

Should I do a patch test?

Yes. We recommend a patch test before using any new product, especially if you have sensitive skin or allergies.

Can I become a seller?

Yes! 150+ people are already part of our business community. Visit the Business Community page and apply.
