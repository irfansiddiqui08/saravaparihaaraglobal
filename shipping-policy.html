Shipping Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Where we ship

We ship across India. International orders (including the UAE, Canada and Gulf countries) are accepted on request. Please message us on WhatsApp for international charges and timelines.

Order confirmation

Orders are placed and confirmed on WhatsApp. Your order is processed once our team has confirmed the items, delivery address and payment with you.

Dispatch and delivery

Confirmed orders are usually dispatched within 1–3 working days. Delivery normally takes 3–7 working days depending on your location. Remote areas may take longer. We share tracking details on WhatsApp once your order is shipped.
