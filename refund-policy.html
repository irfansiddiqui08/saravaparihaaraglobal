Returns & Refunds

Last updated: November 2026

Because our products are personal-care items, we cannot accept returns of opened or used products for hygiene reasons.

Damaged, wrong or missing items

If your parcel arrives damaged, or you receive a wrong or missing item, please message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery with photos and, if possible, an unboxing video. After checking, we will send a replacement or issue a refund.

Refunds

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method, usually within 5–7 working days of approval.

Cancellations

You can cancel an order before it is dispatched by messaging us on WhatsApp.
