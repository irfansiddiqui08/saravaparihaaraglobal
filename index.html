Our story

It started at 13, with a search for answers

At 13, Humayra faced hormonal challenges that affected her hair and her confidence. Instead of giving up, she became curious: about her hair, her scalp and the ingredients she was using.

In 2020 she created a hair oil for herself. It was never meant to be a company. When she saw positive changes in her own hair, she asked: what if this journey could help others too? That question became Sarava Parihaara.

“Listen. Learn. Create. Improve.”
