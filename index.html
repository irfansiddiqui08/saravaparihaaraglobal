Humaira's Journey

Driven by a deep passion for natural healing and authentic Ayurvedic research, Humaira founded Sarava Parihaara to provide a genuine, chemical-free solution to modern hair problems.

After years of meticulous research into the ancient texts of Ayurveda, she formulated the perfect balance of Amla, Bhringraj, and rare potent herbs. Her mission is simple: to offer products that don't just mask hair issues, but heal the scalp from the roots up.
