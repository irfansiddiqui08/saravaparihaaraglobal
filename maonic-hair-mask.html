Maonic is a herbal hair-care blend you can use two ways: soaked overnight as a hair rinse (tonic), or ground with sour curd into a nourishing mask. It brings together six herbs from the Indian kitchen and garden.

  • 2-in-1: use as a tonic or a mask
  • Nourishes scalp and hair lengths
  • Helps hair feel thicker and softer
  • Pure herbal blend

Key ingredients

AmlaRiceReethaHibiscusRosemaryNigella
Ritual

How to use

  1. As a tonic: add 2 teaspoons of Maonic to 200 ml water in a glass vessel and keep overnight.
  2. Next day, after shampoo, rinse your hair with the Maonic water. Do not rinse it out. Use on alternate days.
  3. As a mask: grind soaked Maonic with sour curd into a paste.
  4. Apply on scalp and hair, leave for 1 hour, then wash with Sarava Parihaara shampoo.
Good to know

Care & precautions

  • For external use only.
  • Do a patch test before first use.
  • Store in a cool, dry place.
